# Expert consultation checklist — PENDING EXPERT VERIFICATION

Rubric under review: `tamban-belly-draft-v0.1`. Record the expert's identity, actual consultation date, source reference, and decision separately when the consultation happens; none is assumed here.

1. Which belly openings are routine, acceptable preparation, and which visible or physical findings qualify as a defect? Can photographs distinguish these reliably, or are other views or physical inspection required?
2. What differentiates a hole, tear, exposed tissue and normal processing opening? Can more than one type apply, and how should uncertain cases be recorded?
3. How should `minor`, `moderate` and `severe` be defined and measured? Are there any accepted size, location, extent or other criteria, and how should image scale be calibrated? Do not invent numerical boundaries.
4. For each confirmed finding and severity, may the fish qualify for Class A, B, or C? When is downgrade appropriate and when, if ever, is Rejected required? How do multiple findings interact? Record the reasoning and exceptions.
5. Can the cause (accidental tearing or normal preparation) be established from an image? What evidence is necessary and when must the cause remain uncertain?
6. What evidence confirms a physically missing head or tail? What visibility and physical-inspection requirements distinguish confirmed absence from occlusion or nondetection, and do head and tail absence have different grading consequences?
7. What image quality, viewpoints, belly visibility, occlusion and specimen provenance are required to mark a case as no defect? When should a case be unassessable?
8. What location should annotators outline: the opening, the damaged margin, exposed tissue, or a combination? Are polygons or boxes suitable, and how should partly visible and multiple findings be marked?
9. Which cases require independent second review and adjudication? What qualifications, disagreement rules, audit trail and re-review procedure should be used?
10. Can the expert supply and approve de-identified examples, including acceptable processed openings, uncertain cases and counterexamples, with independently verified specimen IDs and permissions? How should inter-annotator agreement and dataset independence be evaluated before training?

Do not use the draft for detector training or grade-changing automation until the relevant expert answers and the reviewed annotation protocol are recorded.
