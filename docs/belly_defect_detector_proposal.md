These prerequisites were not established in the inspected files. This is a proposal, not a trained detector, implementation, validation result, grading rule or performance claim.

## Candidate approach after prerequisites are met

1. Use the expert-approved rubric to define which findings and negative controls are meaningful, including normal preparation openings, invisible or occluded bellies, and uncertain observations. Preserve independent reviews and adjudications. Obtain permissions and record image/camera/specimen/capture lineage.
2. Compare localized segmentation, a small bounding-box object detector, and a fish-crop classifier on a common specimen/capture-group-isolated study. Segmentation can describe irregular openings but needs more detailed expert masks; bounding boxes are a practical smaller first localization baseline if experts agree; crop classification cannot localize a finding and is unsuitable as the sole ownership signal. Final selection depends on the expert-approved annotation form and data, not this proposal.
3. Resolve detections against every visible parent fish, including unscheduled and untracked competitors. Reuse the existing parent-part association principles, but validate ownership for defect regions specifically. Ambiguous overlap, an invisible belly, or a missing parent must yield uncertain/unassigned evidence rather than transfer a defect or assert intact anatomy.
4. Before training, freeze specimen/capture/augmentation groups and check checkpoint lineage against the validation/test fish. Apply augmentation only to training images, use fixed seeds, retain a separate untouched final test, and predefine class counts and control coverage. A grouped split alone cannot prove independence from undocumented exposure or related captures.
5. Evaluate defect precision/recall with real denominators, localization overlap where appropriate, false positives on confirmed acceptable processing openings, performance on uncertain or occluded cases, parent-association errors and measured inference latency. Report per-group and lighting limitations; do not invent numerical acceptance thresholds. Select any thresholds using training/validation only.
6. If a detector is eventually justified, emit a separate typed experimental `defect_evidence` record containing source/model version, parent identity or unassigned state, localized region, confidence, visibility, and uncertainty. Model output is not physical confirmation. Keep it out of the live A/B/C/Rejected decision until expert criteria and a separate end-to-end validation support a reviewed integration proposal.

## Required to resume

Provide the expert-approved rubric and consultation record, the paths to independently reviewed localized positives and acceptable-opening negative controls, reliable specimen/capture and augmentation lineage, and a documented independent validation split. The available [expert checklist](belly_defect_expert_checklist.md) lists the unresolved questions, and the [annotation pipeline](belly_defect_pipeline.md) can validate a new reviewed batch. Until those inputs exist, the requested detector phase cannot proceed and subsequent sequential prompts remain unstarted.
